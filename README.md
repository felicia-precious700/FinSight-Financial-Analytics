FinSight | Financial Performance & Profitability Analysis

Project Overview

FinSight is an interactive financial analysis project developed in Microsoft Power BI to examine a critical business performance challenge: the disconnect between revenue growth and declining profitability.

Using historical financial transaction data, the project investigates how revenue, direct costs and operating expenditure interact to influence the company's overall financial position.

The analysis goes beyond reporting financial totals by examining cost behaviour across accounts, departments and branches to identify areas contributing to the company's profitability challenges.

Through data transformation, structured data modelling, DAX-based financial measures and interactive visualisations, FinSight converts raw financial records into meaningful business intelligence.

The project provides insight into where revenue is being absorbed, which cost areas require management attention, and why increasing sales do not necessarily translate into stronger financial performance.

Central Business Question

Why is revenue growing while profit margins are shrinking?

Analytical Focus

- Revenue performance and growth patterns.
- Gross profit and cost of sales behaviour.
- Operating expenditure across departments, branches and accounts.
- Profit Before Tax and profitability margins.
- Year-on-Year (YoY) and Month-on-Month (MoM) revenue analysis.
- Expense breakdown and identification of significant cost drivers.
- Areas requiring management attention.

Tools and Methodology

- Microsoft Power BI Desktop: Financial reporting and interactive visualisation.
- Power Query: Data preparation, cleaning and transformation.
- DAX: Financial measures, profitability calculations and time intelligence.
- Data Modelling: Star schema using fact and dimension tables.
- Microsoft Excel: Source financial transaction data.

Key Financial Findings

The analysis produced the following results for the reporting period:

- Total Revenue: Approximately $3.56 billion.
- Total Cost of Sales: Approximately $3.08 billion.
- Gross Profit: Approximately $487.06 million.
- Total Operating Expenses: Approximately $2.47 billion.
- Operating Profit: Approximately -$1.98 billion.
- Profit Before Tax: Approximately -$2.07 billion.
- PBT Margin: -58.01%.

Key Observations

1. Revenue growth and declining gross margin: Revenue increased from approximately $116.75 million in February 2024 to $251.18 million in December 2025. Over these comparison points, gross profit margin declined from 45.82% to 8.09%.

2. High cost of sales: Cost of Goods Sold – Finished Goods contributed approximately $2.18 billion, representing about 71% of total cost of sales.

3. Significant operating expenditure: The Facilities department accounted for approximately $1.05 billion, or 42.4% of total operating expenses. Diesel was the largest individual operating expense account, at approximately $170.70 million.

4. Branch expense concentration: Lagos Mainland recorded the highest operating expenses among the branches analysed.

These findings indicate that revenue growth is not translating into sustainable profitability.

Strategic Recommendations

1. Strengthen cost-of-sales management: Review procurement costs, supplier pricing, importation expenses and inventory management, with particular attention to the cost of finished goods.

2. Improve departmental expense accountability: Review expenditure across departments and branches, prioritising Facilities and significant cost items such as diesel. Introduce departmental budgets and regular spending reviews.

3. Establish monthly financial performance monitoring: Monitor revenue growth, gross profit margin, operating expenses and Profit Before Tax regularly to identify unfavourable trends and support timely management decisions.

Project Deliverables

- Cleansed and modelled Power BI data model.
- Standardised Profit & Loss financial statement matrix.
- Executive business dashboard with YoY and MoM analysis and expense breakdown visuals.
- Management narrative containing key findings and three strategic recommendations.
- Original Excel source workbook and supporting project documentation.

Project Outcome

FinSight demonstrates how financial transaction data can be transformed into an interactive analytical report that explains the relationship between business growth, cost behaviour and profitability.

The project provides evidence-based insights to support more informed financial and operational decisions, with a focus on cost control, expense accountability and sustainable business performance.
