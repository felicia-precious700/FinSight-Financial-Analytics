FinSight | Financial Performance & Profitability Analysis.

Project Overview:

FinSight is an interactive financial analysis project developed in Microsoft Power BI to examine a critical business performance challenge: the disconnect between revenue growth and declining profitability.

Using historical financial transaction data, the project investigates how revenue, direct costs and operating expenditure interact to influence the company's overall financial position. It goes beyond reporting financial totals by examining cost behaviour across accounts, departments and branches to identify areas contributing to the company's profitability challenges.

Through data transformation, structured data modelling, DAX-based financial measures and interactive visualisations, FinSight converts raw financial records into meaningful business intelligence.

The analysis provides a clearer understanding of where revenue is being absorbed, which cost areas require management attention, and why increasing sales do not necessarily translate into stronger financial performance.

Central Business Question

Why is revenue growing while profit margins are shrinking?

Analytical Focus:

- Revenue performance and growth patterns.
- Gross profit and cost of sales behaviour.
- Operating expenditure across departments, branches and accounts.
- Profit before tax and profitability margins.
- Identification of significant cost drivers and areas requiring management attention.

Tools and Methodology:

- Microsoft Power BI Desktop: Financial reporting and interactive visualisation.
- Power Query: Data preparation and transformation.
- DAX: Financial measures, profitability calculations and year-on-year analysis.
- Data Modelling: Star schema with fact and dimension tables.
- Microsoft Excel: Source data.

Project Outcome:

The project demonstrates how financial transaction data can be transformed into an interactive analytical report that explains the relationship between business growth, cost behaviour and profitability, providing evidence to support more informed financial and operational decisions.
